Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about StarFilm — from AI shorts generation to voiceovers and platform support.

How does StarFilm work?

+

StarFilm is an AI-powered shorts generator. Simply enter a topic or story idea, choose your niche, video format (YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reel / TikTok), and voice language. Our AI then:

✅ Writes an emotional, engaging script (hook + body + CTA)
✅ Generates a realistic voiceover using neural TTS
✅ Creates 10 cinematic vertical images (9:16)
✅ Compiles everything into a ready-to-publish video with smooth transitions and background audio.

The whole process takes under 15 minutes — you just download and share!

Can I create YouTube Shorts automatically?

+

Absolutely. StarFilm is built specifically for short-form vertical video platforms. When you select "YouTube Shorts" as the format, our AI optimizes the aspect ratio (9:16), timing (under 60 seconds), and metadata — including an attention‑grabbing title, description, and hashtags tailored for YouTube’s algorithm. You get a ready‑to‑upload MP4 file.

Does StarFilm generate AI voiceovers?

+

Yes! StarFilm uses high‑quality neural text‑to‑speech engines (Edge‑TTS with ElevenLabs fallback). You can choose between multiple voices — Hindi Female/Male, English Female/Male — all natural and emotion‑aware. The voiceover matches the script’s pacing and emotional tone, making your short feel professional and engaging.

Can I create TikTok videos with AI?

+

Definitely. StarFilm supports TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. When you select TikTok, we adjust the video parameters (9:16 vertical, high bitrate, fast pacing) to fit TikTok’s trending style. You get a watermark‑free MP4 that’s ready to upload directly from your phone or desktop. Many creators use our AI shorts to go viral daily.

Do I own the videos I create?

+

100% yours. You retain full ownership of every script, voiceover, and final video generated on StarFilm. We do not claim any rights over your content. You can monetize, repurpose, or delete your videos anytime. Your data is never sold to third parties.

Is StarFilm free to use?

+

Currently, StarFilm is completely free with fair usage limits. You can generate unlimited videos for personal or commercial use. In the future, if premium tiers are introduced (higher resolution, longer videos, team features), we will notify you in advance. No hidden charges, no credit card required at the moment.

How long does it take to generate a video?

+

Typically between 2 to 12 minutes, depending on server load and the complexity of the story. The progress bar updates in real time, showing each step: script writing → voice generation → image creation → video compilation. Most videos are ready in under 10 minutes. You can close the page and come back — the job continues in the background.

Can I customize the script or edit the video?

+

Right now StarFilm delivers a fully automated output. However, you can copy the generated script from the result panel and modify it before re‑generating. For advanced editing, we recommend downloading the video and using tools like CapCut, Adobe Premiere Rush, or DaVinci Resolve. We are working on an interactive script editor for future releases.
