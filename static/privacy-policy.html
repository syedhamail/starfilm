Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

StarFilm collects only the essential data required to provide our AI video generation service. This includes:

  • Account information — email address, name, and secure password hash (never stored in plain text).
  • Content you create — topics, scripts, voice preferences, and generated videos are processed and stored to deliver your results.
  • Usage data — timestamps of video generations, preferred voice settings, and anonymous analytics to improve our AI models.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data powers your creative journey on StarFilm. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Generate scripts, voiceovers, and final video compilations based on your topic.
  • Maintain your account and video history across sessions.
  • Improve the quality and relevance of our AI outputs (only in aggregated, anonymized form).
  • Send important service notifications (password reset, new features) — only with your consent.

We never sell your personal data to third parties or use your videos for advertising without explicit permission.

3. Data Storage & Security

All account details, scripts, and metadata are stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database with encryption at rest. Generated voice files and final videos are uploaded to Cloudinary and Rendi with secure URLs. We use industry‑standard security measures (HTTPS, hashed passwords, session tokens) to prevent unauthorized access.

Your generated videos are stored for as long as your account is active. You can delete any video at any time from your dashboard. Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently erased within 30 days.

4. Third‑Party Services

StarFilm relies on trusted external APIs to deliver high‑quality results:

  • Groq (Llama) — script writing and metadata generation.
  • Edge‑TTS / ElevenLabs — voice synthesis.
  • Pollinations.ai — image generation.
  • Rendi.dev & Cloudinary — video compilation and cloud storage.

Each provider operates under strict data protection policies and only processes the data necessary for the specific task (e.g., your topic prompt). We do not share identifiable user information with these services beyond what is required.

5. Your Rights

As a StarFilm user, you have complete control over your information:

  • Access & correction — view your stored data via dashboard and update your profile.
  • Data export — request a copy of all your generated videos and metadata by contacting support.
  • Deletion — delete individual videos or your entire account directly from settings.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using our service and request data removal at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hamailsyed139@gmail.com.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We only use essential cookies: a session cookie (`sf_session`) to keep you logged in. No tracking pixels, analytics cookies, or third‑party ad trackers are deployed. You can disable cookies in your browser, but the login functionality will be affected.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect new features or legal requirements. If significant changes occur, we will notify you via email or a prominent notice on the website. Continued use of StarFilm after the update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy, please reach out:

  • Email: hamailsyed139@gmail.com
  • Through the official contact form on our website (coming soon).
