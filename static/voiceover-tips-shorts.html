How to Choose the Perfect Voiceover for Your Shorts

February 28, 2026 • 3 min read

The voiceover can make or break your short video. A dull, robotic voice kills retention. A warm, emotional voice keeps viewers watching till the end. Here’s how to choose the right AI voice for your content.

Male vs Female Voice?

No rule says one is better. Test both with your audience. For motivational stories, deeper male voices (like Hindi Madhur) work well. For emotional tales, a soft female voice (like Hindi Swara) often feels more empathetic.

Language Matters

If your target audience is primarily Hindi‑speaking, use Hindi voices. English works for global reach. StarFilm offers both.

Pacing and Emotion

Pick a voice that matches your story’s tempo. Fast, energetic for comedy; slow and deliberate for sad stories. Our AI automatically adjusts to the script’s emotional arcs.
